# M2716 — Intel/SGS-Thomson 2716 16K (2K×8) UV-Erasable EPROM

## Overview
The 2716 (Intel original; SGS-Thomson M2716; equivalent second-sources from AMD, NEC, Fujitsu, etc.) is a 16,384-bit UV-erasable and electrically programmable read-only memory organised as 2,048 words of 8 bits. It operates from a single +5 V supply during normal reads, making it directly compatible with Z80 and 8080-family systems. Programming requires a 25 V programming supply on VPP. The chip is erased by exposure to shortwave (2537 Å) ultraviolet light through the quartz window; a total UV dosage of 15 Ws/cm² is required for a full erase (typically 20–30 minutes with a standard UV lamp at 2.5 cm). The 24-pin DIP package with a transparent window lid is the standard form factor. Access time is 450 ns (standard) or 350 ns (M2716-1).

## Notes
- There are 11 address lines (A0–A10) selecting 2,048 byte locations.
- Three operating modes: (1) Read — EP low, G low, VPP = 5 V; (2) Standby — EP high, outputs Hi-Z, power reduced to ~25 mA; (3) Deselect — G high, outputs Hi-Z independent of EP.
- For programming, VCC and VPP must both be at +5 V and +25 V respectively before EP goes low; the program pulse is 45–55 ms on EP. Multiple 2716s can be programmed simultaneously with different data by controlling individual EP lines while sharing address and data buses.
- The outputs are TRI-STATE and can be wire-OR'd for memory expansion; any number of devices can share the same data bus provided only one EP and G are asserted at a time.
- Direct Intel 2716 equivalents include AMD AM2716, NEC D2716, Fujitsu MBM2716, Motorola MCM2716, and the SGS-Thomson M2716; all are pin-for-pin compatible.

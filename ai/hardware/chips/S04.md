# 74S04 — Hex Inverter (Schottky)

## Overview
The 74S04 contains six independent single-input inverting gates (NOT gates) implemented in the Schottky TTL (74S) process. Schottky clamping diodes prevent transistors from saturating deeply, resulting in significantly faster switching than standard 74LS devices at the cost of higher quiescent power dissipation. The logic function is identical to the 74LS04 and the pinout is fully compatible; the 74S04 is a drop-in speed upgrade used where propagation delay is critical — for example in high-frequency clock buffering or fast-edge signal conditioning in Z80 systems running at the top of their specified clock range.

## Notes
- Typical propagation delay is 3 ns, compared to ~15 ns for 74LS04 and ~9 ns for 74F04.
- Quiescent power dissipation is approximately 600 mW per package (full load), considerably higher than the 74LS04 (~90 mW).
- Fully pin-compatible with 74LS04, 74S04, 74F04, and 74HC04; can replace any of these in a circuit if the supply current budget allows.
- The 74S04 is typically found in the clock path of older Z80 designs where the crystal oscillator, clock buffer, and phase-inversion chain require minimal delay to stay within setup/hold margins.
- Input voltage levels and output drive specifications are standard TTL; fan-out is 10 LS loads per output.

# 74S74 — Dual D-Type Positive-Edge-Triggered Flip-Flop (Schottky)

## Overview
The 74S74 contains two independent positive-edge-triggered D-type flip-flops implemented in the Schottky TTL (74S) process, providing significantly faster switching than the equivalent 74LS74. Each flip-flop has an individual data input (D), clock input (CLK), asynchronous active-LOW preset (~PRE), asynchronous active-LOW clear (~CLR), and complementary Q and ~Q outputs. Logic function and pinout are identical to the 74LS74. The 74S74 is used where clock-to-Q propagation delay is critical — for example in fast synchronisers, high-speed dividers, or interrupt-detection circuits in demanding Z80 clock configurations.

## Pinout
| Pin | Name | Type | Description |
|-----|------|------|-------------|
| 1 | ~1CLR | I | Flip-flop 1 asynchronous clear, active LOW |
| 2 | 1D | I | Flip-flop 1 data input |
| 3 | 1CLK | I | Flip-flop 1 clock input (captures D on rising edge) |
| 4 | ~1PRE | I | Flip-flop 1 asynchronous preset, active LOW |
| 5 | 1Q | O | Flip-flop 1 non-inverting output |
| 6 | ~1Q | O | Flip-flop 1 inverting output |
| 7 | GND | P | Ground (0 V) |
| 8 | ~2Q | O | Flip-flop 2 inverting output |
| 9 | 2Q | O | Flip-flop 2 non-inverting output |
| 10 | ~2PRE | I | Flip-flop 2 asynchronous preset, active LOW |
| 11 | 2CLK | I | Flip-flop 2 clock input (captures D on rising edge) |
| 12 | 2D | I | Flip-flop 2 data input |
| 13 | ~2CLR | I | Flip-flop 2 asynchronous clear, active LOW |
| 14 | VCC | P | Supply voltage (+5 V) |

## Notes
- Typical propagation delay from CLK to Q is approximately 8 ns, compared to ~25 ns for the 74LS74.
- Setup time before the clock edge is typically 3 ns; hold time is typically 2 ns.
- Both ~PRE and ~CLR are asynchronous; asserting both simultaneously forces both Q and ~Q HIGH, an indeterminate state to be avoided.
- Power dissipation is higher than the LS variant (~125 mW per package typical) due to the Schottky process.
- Pin-for-pin compatible with 74LS74 and 74F74, allowing direct board-level substitution where faster timing is required.
- In Z80 systems the 74S74 is chosen over the 74LS74 when the flip-flop sits in a fast clock path and timing margins are tight.

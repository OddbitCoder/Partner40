# 74LS132 — Quadruple 2-Input Schmitt-Trigger NAND Gate

## Overview
The 74LS132 contains four independent 2-input NAND gates, each with Schmitt-trigger inputs. The Schmitt-trigger action provides hysteresis: the positive-going input threshold (VT+) is higher than the negative-going threshold (VT−). This makes the device highly immune to noise and slow input transitions, producing clean, jitter-free outputs even when driven by slowly changing or noisy signals. It is electrically and pin-compatible with the 74LS00 but with the added Schmitt-trigger input conditioning.

## Notes
- Typical hysteresis: VT+ ≈ 1.6 V, VT− ≈ 0.8 V (LS subfamily at 5 V, 25 °C), giving ~0.8 V of noise margin.
- Drop-in replacement for the 74LS00 in noise-sensitive circuits; logic function is identical (2-input NAND) but with hysteresis on inputs.
- Commonly used to debounce mechanical switches, condition slow RC-ramp signals, and regenerate degraded TTL clock edges.
- The four gates are fully independent; no shared enables or common control lines.
- Totem-pole outputs (not open-collector); outputs can source and sink current without external pull-up resistors.

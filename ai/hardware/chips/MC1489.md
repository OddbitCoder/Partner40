# MC1489 — Motorola Quad RS-232 Line Receiver

## Overview
The MC1489 (also available as SN75189, DS1489, and equivalent second-sources) is a monolithic quad line receiver in a 14-pin DIP package, designed to convert RS-232-C / EIA-232D signal levels back to TTL/DTL-compatible logic levels. It contains four independent receiver channels (A, B, C, D), each with a single RS-232 input, a response-control pin for hysteresis and noise-filter adjustment, and a TTL-compatible output. Each receiver incorporates built-in hysteresis (threshold typically at +1.2 V and −1.2 V), providing noise immunity for RS-232 lines. The response-control pin of each channel can be connected to a capacitor to ground to set a noise-rejection time constant, or to a voltage divider to shift the input threshold. The MC1489A variant has tighter threshold specifications. The device operates from a single +5 V supply.

## Pinout

| Pin | Name | Type | Description |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | IN A | I | RS-232 Input of Receiver A. Accepts signals in the range ±30 V; threshold nominally ±1.2 V. |
| 2 | CTRL A | I | Response Control A. Connect to ground (or leave open) for standard operation; connect a capacitor to ground to slow response and reject noise pulses; connect to a voltage to shift the input threshold. |
| 3 | OUT A | O | TTL Output of Receiver A. High when input is more negative than the negative threshold; low when input is more positive than the positive threshold. |
| 4 | IN B | I | RS-232 Input of Receiver B. |
| 5 | CTRL B | I | Response Control B. |
| 6 | OUT B | O | TTL Output of Receiver B. |
| 7 | GND | P | Ground (0 V). |
| 8 | OUT C | O | TTL Output of Receiver C. |
| 9 | CTRL C | I | Response Control C. |
| 10 | IN C | I | RS-232 Input of Receiver C. |
| 11 | OUT D | O | TTL Output of Receiver D. |
| 12 | CTRL D | I | Response Control D. |
| 13 | IN D | I | RS-232 Input of Receiver D. |
| 14 | VCC | P | Positive Supply (+5 V ±10%). |

## Notes
- The MC1489 is the complementary receiver to the MC1488 line driver; they are designed to be used together.
- Input voltage range is ±30 V, far exceeding the ±15 V RS-232 specification, providing protection against over-voltage conditions on communication lines.
- Built-in hysteresis (typically 1.2 V) makes the device inherently noise-immune without additional filtering.
- The response-control pin (CTRL) can be left open for default operation. A capacitor from CTRL to GND (typically 1–10 nF) slows the response time to reject short-duration noise glitches. A resistor voltage divider to CTRL shifts the input threshold.
- The MC1489A variant offers tighter guaranteed threshold specifications (input threshold voltage 0.75 V to 3.0 V).
- Second-source equivalents include Texas Instruments SN75189/SN75189A, National Semiconductor DS1489, and Signetics NE1489.
- Single +5 V supply operation makes the MC1489 simpler to power than the MC1488 (which requires ±12 V).

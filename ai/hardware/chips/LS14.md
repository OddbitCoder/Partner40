# 74LS14 — Hex Schmitt-Trigger Inverter

## Overview
The 74LS14 contains six independent single-input inverters, each with a Schmitt-trigger input. Unlike standard TTL inverters, each gate has two separate switching thresholds: a higher positive-going threshold (V_T+, typically 1.6 V) and a lower negative-going threshold (V_T−, typically 0.8 V). The resulting hysteresis (~0.8 V) makes the device immune to slow-moving, noisy, or marginally-swinging signals, converting them into clean, fast digital transitions. The 74LS14 is functionally a drop-in replacement for the 74LS04 in circuits requiring improved noise rejection.

## Notes
- The Schmitt-trigger hysteresis (~0.8 V typical) prevents multiple output transitions when the input signal crosses the threshold slowly or contains noise.
- Typical positive-going threshold V_T+ ≈ 1.6 V; negative-going threshold V_T− ≈ 0.8 V.
- Pin-compatible with the 74LS04 (hex inverter without Schmitt triggers), allowing direct substitution where noise immunity must be improved.
- Commonly used for switch/contact debouncing, RC oscillator circuits, signal conditioning of slow edges (e.g., crystal oscillators, long PCB traces), and RESET line filtering.
- Propagation delay is typically 15 ns at 5 V.

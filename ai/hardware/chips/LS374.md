# 74LS374 — Octal D-Type Edge-Triggered Flip-Flop with 3-State Outputs

## Overview
The 74LS374 contains eight positive-edge-triggered D-type flip-flops whose Q outputs are gated by a common active-LOW output-enable (~OC). On each rising edge of the shared clock (CLK), all eight Q outputs are updated to reflect the logic levels present at the D inputs at that instant. When ~OC is HIGH, all Q outputs float to high impedance, enabling the device to connect directly to a shared data bus. The 74LS374 is used extensively in Z80 systems as an 8-bit output latch for data-bus isolation, address latching, and I/O port registers.

## Notes
- Data capture occurs on the LOW-to-HIGH transition of CLK; D inputs must be stable at least 20 ns before the clock edge (setup time) and 5 ns after (hold time).
- The ~OC output-enable does not affect the stored state of the flip-flops; data continues to be clocked normally even when outputs are in high-impedance mode.
- The closely related 74LS373 is a latch (transparent, level-sensitive) rather than an edge-triggered flip-flop, sharing the same pinout.
- In Z80 systems the LS374 is commonly used as an I/O output port: the Z80's ~WR and a decoded address strobe together clock the flip-flops, while ~OC may be tied permanently LOW or controlled for bus sharing.
- Maximum clock frequency is typically 25 MHz.

# 74LS04 — Hex Inverter

## Overview
The 74LS04 contains six independent single-input inverting gates (NOT gates). The output of each gate is the logical complement of its input: a HIGH input produces a LOW output and vice versa. Outputs are standard push-pull (totem-pole) TTL with no 3-state or open-collector features. The hex inverter is among the most widely used logic chips in any digital design and appears in virtually every Z80-based computer for bus polarity inversion, active-level conversion, clock buffering, and oscillator feedback.

## Notes
- Typical propagation delay is 15 ns (LS subfamily).
- The 74LS14 is pin-compatible but adds Schmitt-trigger hysteresis to each input for improved noise immunity.
- The 74LS05 is pin-compatible but has open-collector outputs, requiring external pull-up resistors.
- Two inverters in series produce a non-inverting buffer with a small delay; this technique is used when a standard non-inverting buffer chip is unavailable.
- In Z80 systems, the LS04 commonly inverts active-LOW signals to active-HIGH and vice versa, and is used in the oscillator/clock circuit together with a crystal.

# 74LS05 — Hex Inverter with Open-Collector Outputs

## Overview
The 74LS05 contains six independent single-input inverter (NOT) gates, each with an open-collector output. Because the outputs are open-collector rather than totem-pole, an external pull-up resistor is required on each output line. This makes the device suitable for wired-AND configurations, level shifting to higher voltages, and driving LEDs or other loads directly.

## Notes
- Each output requires an external pull-up resistor (typically 1 kΩ–10 kΩ to VCC) to assert a logic HIGH.
- Open-collector outputs may be wire-ANDed together on a shared bus; the bus is LOW if any driver pulls it low.
- Maximum collector voltage on outputs is 5.5 V; maximum sink current is 8 mA (LS subfamily).
- Functionally equivalent to the 74LS04 except for the open-collector output structure.
- The 74LS06 is a higher-voltage (30 V) open-collector variant intended for interfacing to higher-level logic or relay drivers.

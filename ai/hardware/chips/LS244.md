# 74LS244 — Octal Buffer/Line Driver with 3-State Outputs

## Overview
The 74LS244 contains eight independent non-inverting buffer/line drivers arranged as two groups of four, each group sharing a common active-low output-enable (~1G and ~2G). When an enable is asserted LOW, the four corresponding outputs follow their inputs; when deasserted HIGH, those outputs float to a high-impedance (3-state) condition. The chip is designed to drive highly capacitive or low-impedance bus lines and is widely used as a unidirectional data/address bus driver in Z80 and similar microcomputer systems.

## Notes
- All outputs are 3-state (high-impedance when enable is deasserted), making this device suitable for bidirectional bus architectures where multiple drivers share a bus.
- The two enable pins (~1G and ~2G) allow the chip to act as two independent 4-bit buffers or as a single 8-bit buffer when both enables are tied together.
- Commonly used to buffer the Z80 address bus, data bus, or control lines toward peripheral devices.
- A logic HIGH on an enable pin disables (floats) that group of outputs; a logic LOW enables them.
- Propagation delay is typically 12 ns; output enable/disable time is typically 18 ns.

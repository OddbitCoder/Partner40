# Z80A SIO/1 — Z8441 Serial Input/Output Controller

## Overview
The Z80A SIO/1 (Zilog Z8441) is a dual-channel serial communications controller in a 40-pin DIP package. It provides two independent full-duplex serial channels (A and B) supporting synchronous and asynchronous data formats including SDLC/HDLC, IBM Bisync, and monosync. Each channel has a dedicated baud-rate clock input for both transmit and receive, and supports programmable character lengths, parity, and stop bits in async mode. The SIO/1 variant is distinguished from SIO/0 (Z8440) and SIO/2 (Z8442) by its modem-control pin assignments: it provides W/RDYA and W/RDYB (Wait/Ready) outputs on both channels but omits DTRB, whereas SIO/0 provides full DTR and DCD modem signals on both channels and SIO/2 omits modem signals entirely. The W/RDY pins can be programmed as either CPU wait signals or DMA ready signals. All active-low signals are denoted with a leading slash.

## Notes
- The SIO/1 (Z8441) differs from SIO/0 (Z8440) in that channel B omits DTRB (present on SIO/0 pin 39) and instead provides W/RDYB on both channels; channel A retains /DTRA and /DCDA as on SIO/0.
- The SIO/2 (Z8442) omits all modem control signals on both channels; the SIO/1 is a compromise retaining full modem control on channel A.
- A/B and C/D are typically connected to address lines A1 and A0 respectively, giving four addressable registers (channel A data, channel A control, channel B data, channel B control).
- Both TxC and RxC on each channel can accept an external baud-rate clock, typically generated by a Z80 CTC channel whose ZC/TO output feeds the SIO clock input.
- The W/RDY pins are open-drain when configured as WAIT outputs, allowing them to be wire-ORed with other WAIT sources on the system bus.
- SDLC/HDLC mode provides automatic zero-insertion (bit stuffing), FCS generation and checking, and abort sequence detection.
- Each channel's interrupt vector is based on the vector register and the source (Rx, Tx, or special condition), with channel A having higher interrupt priority than channel B within the chip.

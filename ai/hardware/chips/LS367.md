# 74LS367 — Hex Buffer/Line Driver with 3-State Outputs

## Overview
The 74LS367 contains six independent non-inverting buffer/line drivers with 3-state outputs. The six buffers are split into two groups: four buffers share one active-low enable (~1OE, controlling buffers 1–4) and two buffers share a second active-low enable (~2OE, controlling buffers 5–6). When an enable is LOW the corresponding outputs follow their inputs; when HIGH, those outputs float to high impedance. The device is commonly used for driving bus lines while allowing orderly sharing of a common data or address bus.

## Pinout
| Pin | Name | Type | Description |
|-----|------|------|-------------|
| 1 | ~1OE | I | Group 1 output enable (buffers 1–4), active LOW |
| 2 | 1A | I | Buffer 1 input |
| 3 | 1Y | O | Buffer 1 output |
| 4 | 2A | I | Buffer 2 input |
| 5 | 2Y | O | Buffer 2 output |
| 6 | 3A | I | Buffer 3 input |
| 7 | 3Y | O | Buffer 3 output |
| 8 | GND | P | Ground (0 V) |
| 9 | 4Y | O | Buffer 4 output |
| 10 | 4A | I | Buffer 4 input |
| 11 | 5Y | O | Buffer 5 output |
| 12 | 5A | I | Buffer 5 input |
| 13 | 6Y | O | Buffer 6 output |
| 14 | 6A | I | Buffer 6 input |
| 15 | ~2OE | I | Group 2 output enable (buffers 5–6), active LOW |
| 16 | VCC | P | Supply voltage (+5 V) |

## Notes
- The asymmetric grouping (4 + 2) reflects a common use case where four of the six drivers serve a nibble-wide bus and the remaining two handle separate control or address lines.
- The closely related 74LS368 is identical except its outputs are inverting.
- Unlike the 74LS244, which has a 20-pin package for eight channels, the LS367 provides only six channels in a 16-pin package with correspondingly lower pin count.
- In Z80 systems the LS367 often buffers the upper address lines (A8–A13) or MEMREQ/IORQ/RD/WR control signals toward the expansion bus.

# LS629 — 74LS629 Dual Voltage-Controlled Oscillator

## Overview
The SN74LS629 (Texas Instruments; also Motorola MC74LS629, Signetics N74LS629, and other second-sources) is a dual voltage-controlled oscillator (VCO) integrated circuit in a 16-pin DIP package. Each of the two independent VCOs produces a square-wave output whose frequency is set by an external capacitor (or crystal) and controlled by two analogue voltage inputs: a frequency-control input (FREQ) and a frequency-range input (RNG). Raising the voltage on FREQ increases frequency; RNG sets the coarse frequency range. An active-low enable input (EN) gates each VCO output. The device has separate supply and ground pins for the oscillator section (OSC VCC, OSC GND) and the output/enable section (VCC, GND), allowing isolation of sensitive analogue oscillator circuitry from digital switching noise. Maximum output frequency is approximately 20 MHz. The 74LS629 was widely used as the VCO element in floppy-disk data-separator PLL circuits, particularly in conjunction with the WD1691 FSL chip.

## Pinout

| Pin | Name | Type | Description |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | 1RNG | I | Frequency Range Control, VCO 1. Analogue voltage input; higher voltage selects higher frequency range. Nominal input impedance 25 kΩ. |
| 2 | 1CX1 | I | Capacitor/Crystal Connection 1 for VCO 1. One terminal of the external timing capacitor or crystal. |
| 3 | NC | — | No internal connection. |
| 4 | 1CX2 | I | Capacitor/Crystal Connection 2 for VCO 1. Other terminal of the external timing capacitor or crystal (or connect to OSC GND for single-capacitor mode). |
| 5 | 1EN | I | Enable Input, VCO 1 (active low). Low enables the 1Y output; high forces 1Y low. |
| 6 | 1Y | O | Oscillator Output, VCO 1. TTL-compatible square wave at the controlled frequency. |
| 7 | OSC GND | P | Oscillator Ground. Separate ground for the analogue oscillator and frequency-control circuits; decouple closely to OSC VCC. |
| 8 | VCC | P | Positive Supply for output and enable logic section (+5 V). |
| 9 | 2RNG | I | Frequency Range Control, VCO 2. |
| 10 | 2CX1 | I | Capacitor/Crystal Connection 1 for VCO 2. |
| 11 | NC | — | No internal connection. |
| 12 | 2CX2 | I | Capacitor/Crystal Connection 2 for VCO 2. |
| 13 | 2EN | I | Enable Input, VCO 2 (active low). |
| 14 | 2Y | O | Oscillator Output, VCO 2. |
| 15 | OSC VCC | P | Oscillator Positive Supply (+5 V). Separate supply for the analogue oscillator and frequency-control circuits. |
| 16 | GND | P | Ground for output and enable logic section. |

## Notes
- The output frequency is a function of the voltage on both 1FREQ (not a separate pin — the frequency control is applied via the 1RNG and external C combination) and the external timing capacitor value. In practice, a 100–500 pF capacitor is used for floppy-disk data-separator applications targeting 125 kHz (FM) or 250 kHz (MFM) centre frequencies.
- The OSC VCC and OSC GND pins should be decoupled from the VCC/GND pins with separate bypass capacitors (e.g. 0.1 µF ceramic) to prevent oscillator noise from coupling into digital logic.
- In floppy-disk PLL data-separator applications, the WD1691 PU and PD* outputs drive a resistor network on the 1RNG pin to steer the VCO frequency up or down; 1CX1 and 1CX2 are connected to a fixed capacitor to establish the free-running frequency.
- The SN74LS624–SN74LS629 family differs in pin assignments for certain enable and frequency-control signals; the LS629 is the 16-pin variant with both FREQ and RNG control inputs separate.
- Enable inputs (1EN, 2EN) are TTL-compatible; driving them low allows the respective VCO to oscillate freely; driving them high suppresses the output (output remains low).

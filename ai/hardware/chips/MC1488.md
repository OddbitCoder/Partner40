# MC1488 — Motorola Quad RS-232 Line Driver

## Overview
The MC1488 (also available as SN75188, DS1488, and equivalent second-sources) is a monolithic quad line driver in a 14-pin DIP package designed to interface TTL/DTL logic levels to EIA RS-232-C / EIA-232D data communication equipment. It contains four independent driver channels (A, B, C, D). Channels B, C, and D each have two logic inputs that are internally NOR'd before driving the output, allowing wired-AND gating without external logic. Channel A has a single input. All outputs produce standard RS-232 signal levels (nominally ±9 V to ±15 V swing) from dual ±12 V (or ±15 V) supply voltages. The outputs are capable of driving cable capacitances up to 2,500 pF.

## Pinout

| Pin | Name | Type | Description |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | VEE | P | Negative Supply Voltage (typically −12 V or −15 V). Provides the negative RS-232 output swing. |
| 2 | IN A | I | Input of Driver A (single input). TTL/DTL-compatible; active high drives output positive. |
| 3 | OUT A | O | Output of Driver A. RS-232 level output; positive for high input, negative for low input. |
| 4 | IN B1 | I | First input of Driver B. Internally NOR'd with IN B2. |
| 5 | IN B2 | I | Second input of Driver B. Internally NOR'd with IN B1. |
| 6 | OUT B | O | Output of Driver B. |
| 7 | OUT C | O | Output of Driver C. |
| 8 | GND | P | Ground (0 V). Signal and logic ground reference. |
| 9 | IN C2 | I | Second input of Driver C. Internally NOR'd with IN C1. |
| 10 | IN C1 | I | First input of Driver C. |
| 11 | OUT D | O | Output of Driver D. |
| 12 | IN D2 | I | Second input of Driver D. Internally NOR'd with IN D1. |
| 13 | IN D1 | I | First input of Driver D. |
| 14 | VCC | P | Positive Supply Voltage (typically +12 V or +15 V). Provides the positive RS-232 output swing. |

## Notes
- The MC1488 is intended to be used with the MC1489 quad line receiver, which provides the complementary RS-232 receiving function.
- Typical supply voltages are ±12 V or ±15 V; the output swing tracks VCC and VEE minus internal transistor drops (approximately ±9 V to ±13 V with ±12 V supplies).
- For channels B, C, and D: the output is positive (logic "space") when any input is high (NOR logic). Both inputs low = negative output (logic "mark").
- Maximum output source/sink current is approximately 10 mA; outputs can drive RS-232 cables with up to 2,500 pF capacitance.
- The device is compatible with EIA-232D, CCITT V.28, and Federal Standard 1030. Second-source equivalents include the Texas Instruments SN75188, National Semiconductor DS1488, and Signetics NE1488.

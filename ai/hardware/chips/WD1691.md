# WD1691 — Floppy Support Logic (F.S.L.)

## Overview
The WD1691 is a 20-pin N-MOS chip designed to minimise the external logic required to interface the WD179X family of Floppy Disk Controllers to a drive. It provides two main functions: (1) a Phase-Lock Loop data separator that generates the RCLK signal for the WD179X by controlling an external VCO via PUMP UP/PUMP DOWN outputs; and (2) a Write Precompensation circuit that works with the WD2143 clock generator to compensate for bit-shift on high-density tracks. It also handles VFOE/WF demultiplexing and supports both 8" (4 MHz VCO) and 5.25" (2 MHz VCO) drives. Single +5V supply.

## Pinout

| Pin | Name | Type | Description |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | WDIN | I | Write Data Input. Ties directly to the FD179X WD pin. |
| 2 | Φ2 | I | Phase 2 clock from WD2143. One of four phase inputs used to generate Write Precompensation delay. Used as the write data pulse on nominal (Early=Late=0). |
| 3 | Φ3 | I | Phase 3 clock from WD2143. Used for Late precompensation. |
| 4 | Φ1 | I | Phase 1 clock from WD2143. Used for Early precompensation. |
| 5 | STB | O | Strobe Output. Latches high on the leading edge of WDIN; resets low on the leading edge of Φ4. Used to trigger pulse generation in the WD2143. |
| 6 | WDOUT | O | Write Data Output. Serial, pre-compensated write data stream sent to the disk drive's WD line. |
| 7 | WG | I | Write Gate. Ties directly to the FD179X WG pin. When high, disables the data recovery circuit. |
| 8 | VFOE/WF | I | VFO Enable / Write Fault. Ties directly to the FD179X VFOE/WF pin. Used to enable/disable the data recovery circuit. |
| 9 | TG43 | I | Track 43. Ties directly to the FD179X TG43 pin. When low, enables Write Precompensation for tracks 44–76. Has internal pull-up; leave open if precompensation is desired on all tracks. |
| 10 | VSS | P | Ground (0V). |
| 11 | RDD | I | Raw Read Data. Composite clock and data stream input from the drive. |
| 12 | RCLK | O | Read Clock. Generated by the WD1691 (VCO ÷ 16 in FM, ÷ 8 in MFM). Ties to the FD179X RCLK pin. |
| 13 | PU | O (3-state) | Pump Up. Tri-state output forced high when the WD1691 requires an increase in VCO frequency. Combine with PD to form a single PUMP signal. |
| 14 | PD | O (3-state) | Pump Down. Tri-state output forced low when the WD1691 requires a decrease in VCO frequency. |
| 15 | DDEN | I | Double Density Enable (active low). When low, selects MFM/double-density mode; VCO is used directly. When high, VCO is divided by two for FM/single-density. Has internal pull-up. |
| 16 | VCO | I | VCO Clock Input. Nominal 4.0 MHz for 8" drives, 2.0 MHz for 5.25" drives. Typically driven by a 74LS629 or 74S124 VCO. |
| 17 | EARLY | I | Early signal from FD179X. Used with LATE to select Write Precompensation phase. |
| 18 | LATE | I | Late signal from FD179X. Used with EARLY to select Write Precompensation phase. |
| 19 | Φ4 | I | Phase 4 clock from WD2143. Resets the STB line in anticipation of the next write data pulse. |
| 20 | VCC | P | +5V power supply (±10%). |

## Notes
- DDEN, TG43, and RDD have internal pull-up resistors; they may be left open for a logic 1.
- PU and PD may be tied together to form a single PUMP signal: forced high for frequency increase, forced low for decrease, tri-stated when no adjustment is needed.
- On the Partner 40, E48 (WD1691) works with E56 (D765 FDC), E57 (WD2143 clock generator), and E70 (LS629 VCO) to form the complete floppy disk interface.
- In single-density-only mode, Φ1–Φ4 and STB should be tied together, DDEN left open, and TG43 tied to ground.

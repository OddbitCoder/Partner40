# 7404 — Hex Inverter

## Overview
The 7404 contains six independent single-input inverter (NOT) gates with standard totem-pole outputs. Each gate inverts its input: a HIGH input produces a LOW output, and a LOW input produces a HIGH output. The 7404 is one of the most fundamental TTL logic devices and is available in the original 7400-series as well as the low-power Schottky variant (74LS04), high-speed CMOS variant (74HC04), and others.

## Notes
- The totem-pole outputs can both source and sink current, unlike open-collector variants (74LS05/74LS06).
- Supply voltage: 4.75 V – 5.25 V (TTL); operating temperature range: 0 °C to 70 °C (commercial), –55 °C to 125 °C (military 5404).
- The 74LS04 (Low-Power Schottky) is the most common replacement, offering lower power consumption with the same pinout.
- Six independent gates allow all six to be used for different signals in the same package; there is no shared enable or common control.
